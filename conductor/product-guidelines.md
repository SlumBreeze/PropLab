# Product Guidelines

## Brand Personality & Tone
PropLab adopts a **Sophisticated and Expert** tone. The language should be authoritative, precise, and understated, positioning the platform as a high-end tool for elite players who value professional-grade insights over hype.

## Visual Identity
*   **Aesthetic:** **Clean and Minimalist.** The UI should prioritize clarity and white space, ensuring that the sophisticated data remains easy to interpret without overwhelming the user.
*   **Theme:** Light mode by default (implied by minimalist preference), focusing on professional typography and subtle accents to denote critical information.

## User Experience Principles
*   **Information Architecture:** **Progressive Disclosure.** The dashboard should provide a streamlined overview of high-value opportunities, allowing users to drill deep into the underlying math and AI analysis only when needed. This approach maintains focus and reduces cognitive load during high-stakes decision-making.
